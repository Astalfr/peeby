# Design

Peeby looks like a Windows 11 app with a neutral dark theme. It takes peeby's visual system as it is (Fluent shapes, Mica chrome, a single accent at a time, discreet motion), the accent being the open space's: YouTube's red, Jellyfin's amber ([ADR 0006](decisions/0006-peeby-visual-system.md), [ADR 0007](decisions/0007-multi-space-app.md)). The values live in `crates/ui/src/theme.rs`. Pages use `theme::…` instead of hard-coded colors, and gpui-component widgets get the same palette through `theme::apply`. Peeby's own description is `D:\DEV\peeby\docs\design.md`.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `base` | `#0B0B0C` | Window chrome without Mica |
| Mica veil | `#0B0B0C` at 55 % | Over Mica, so the chrome stays dark |
| `background` | `#121214` | Content panel, pages |
| `surface` | `#1C1C1F` | Cards, settings rows, description, inputs |
| `surface_hover` | `#27272B` | Hovered surfaces, skeletons |
| `border` | `#2F2F34` | Borders, separators |
| `foreground` | `#F2F2F3` | Text |
| `muted` | `#A1A1AA` | Secondary text |
| `subtle` | `#71717A` | Section labels, fine print |
| `accent` | `#FF4E45` (YouTube), `#FF8A3D` (Jellyfin) | Primary buttons, progress, selection, play buttons, active item |
| `accent_hover` | `#FF6B63`, `#FF9F5E` | Hovered accent elements and titles |
| `accent_soft` | the accent at 14 % | Notice discs, step numbers, selected rows |

One accent at a time, the open space's (`theme::set_accent`; the settings page draws each space's part in its own): it marks what can be acted on or what is selected. Everything else stays neutral, so the thumbnails carry the color.

## Window and layout

- **Logo**: peeby's round logo with a small red play badge (the accent, `#ff4e45`) in a dark ring at the bottom right. `assets/logo.png` (256 px) is drawn in the top bar, scaled on the CPU to the exact device-pixel size (`assets::logo`, GPUI has no mipmaps); `assets/peeby.ico` (16 to 256 px) is embedded in the exe by `build.rs` (`embed-resource`), and GPUI takes the window and taskbar icon from it (resource ID 1).
- **Top bar** (44 px, a window drag area whose controls are occluded), the same in every space: a menu button (burger) that folds the sidebar, the back button, Peeby's mark (the 22 px round logo and the name), then the **spaces as tabs** (34 px high: the service's logo in its own colors, 18 px (`SpaceInfo::icon`, a colored SVG drawn by `ui::assets::color_svg`), and the name; the open tab has a light background, a semibold name and a 2 px line in its accent underneath). On the right: search (opens the open space's search page, tooltip "Rechercher dans YouTube" or "… Jellyfin"; disabled when the space has none, e.g. Jellyfin signed out), the notifications bell and the settings gear, before the window buttons. A video opens from the command line (`peeby <url-or-id>`); there is no URL field.
- **Accent per space**: YouTube red `#FF4E45`, Jellyfin amber `#FF8A3D` (hover `#FF9F5E`, pressed `#E8752A`, peeby's), later Twitch violet. The whole app takes the open space's accent.
- **Notifications** (bell): one 440 px panel, "Notifications", then a section per space that has some, titled with the space's name in its accent; the badge adds up the unread counts. Without any: "Aucune notification…".
- **Mini-player** (ADR 0008): a borderless 400×225 window above every other one, at the bottom right of the screen, with the video alone. On hover: a dark strip at the top that moves the window, with two round 28 px buttons on its right ("Revenir à la page", "Fermer"), a 48 px play/pause button in the middle, and a grip in the bottom right corner that resizes it at 16:9 (240 px wide at least).
- **Sidebar** (232 px): the open space's entries only. YouTube: Accueil, Abonnements, À regarder plus tard (that playlist's page, as a sidebar page) and Playlists (the account is in the settings). Jellyfin: Accueil, then its video libraries (film, tv or folder icon) under a "BIBLIOTHÈQUES" heading (a thin line when folded); pinned at the bottom above a separator, the user (a 24 px avatar, or their initial on a disc in the accent, the name and the server's host on a muted second line; it opens the settings) and "Se déconnecter". Signed out, Jellyfin's sidebar is empty. The subscribed channels are not listed there (hundreds of them), but on their own page. Entries are 40 px, muted until hovered; the entry of the page at the bottom of the stack has a light background and a 3×16 px pill in the accent on its left edge, so Abonnements stays lit on the channels and channel pages opened from it. Folded (burger button, saved as `sidebar_collapsed` in `settings.toml`), it is 56 px wide: each entry shows its icon alone, centered, with its label as a tooltip, and keeps its pill.
- **Content**: a panel with a 12 px top-left corner and a 1 px top and left border, over the chrome as in Windows 11 Settings. Pages have 32 px side margins.
- **Shapes**: 8 px radius for controls, 12 px for cards and large surfaces. Font: Segoe UI Variable Text, else Segoe UI, at 15 px.

## Pages

- **Feeds** (Home, subscriptions, channel): rows of cards at least 416 px wide, as many per row as the content panel holds, widened to fill the row. The page header (title, grid/list switch, "Toutes les chaînes") stays at the top while the videos scroll under it. A card shows the 16:9 thumbnail (duration badge bottom right, or a red "EN DIRECT" badge with a white dot for a live happening now, in every layout: grid, list and compact), the channel avatar, a title of at most two lines, the channel and views · date. Cards keep their natural height and a row aligns them at the top (`h_flex` centers its children by default, which lowered cards with a one-line title). The subscriptions page has a grid/list switch and a "Toutes les chaînes" button.
- **List mode** (subscriptions only, saved as `subscriptions_layout` in `settings.toml`): one row per video as YouTube showed them until about 2023, across the whole page width. A row has a 246 px thumbnail, a two-line title at 18 px, the channel with a 20 px avatar, then views · date. On hover it gets a soft surface (at once, without a transition) and a play button, and the title turns red. There is no description snippet, since the feed does not carry one.
- **Playlists** (sidebar): a grid of 320 px playlist cards. A card shows the first video's thumbnail over two narrower layers peeking out above it (a stack), a count badge with a list icon, the title and its details (privacy, last update). Hover: as video cards.
- **Playlist**: a 320 px column on the left with the cover, the title (2xl, bold), owner and count, and two buttons, **Tout lire** (red) and **Aléatoire**. On the right, the videos as numbered rows (160 px thumbnail, two-line title, channel · views · date), loading the next 100 as the end comes near. A click on a row plays the playlist from there.
- **Queue panel** (watch page of a playlist, above the related videos): a bordered card with the playlist's title, the place (`3 / 17`, `+` while more are to load), shuffle and repeat toggles, then up to 420 px of rows in play order (100 px thumbnails). The current video's row is on `accent_soft` with a red play icon instead of its number, and the panel opens scrolled to it. The player gets previous and next buttons around play/pause (keys P and N), disabled at the ends.
- **Saving to a playlist**:
  - grid cards and list rows show, on hover only, a round **Watch later** button at the thumbnail's top right: a clock, or a check when the video is in "Watch later" already (a click then removes it; the "⋮" menu entry toggles the same way) and a **⋮** button by the title. Their clicks are their own, not the card's;
  - **⋮** opens a popover with "À regarder plus tard" and "Ajouter à une playlist", which turns into the list of playlists;
  - the watch page has a ghost **⋮** button right of the votes, a popover with "Enregistrer dans une playlist", which turns into that list;
  - the list ("Enregistrer dans…") checks the playlists holding the video (a red box with a check), a lock marks private ones; a click adds or removes at once and undoes the check if YouTube refuses;
  - in a playlist the user can edit (their own, "Watch later"), each video of the playlist page and of the queue panel gets an **x** on hover (not the playing one), removing it from the playlist;
  - while a request runs, its "x" or "Watch later" button shows a small spinner and stays visible after the mouse leaves; a second click sends nothing;
  - a playlist's page has a reverse button (arrow-down-up, highlighted when on) next to "Tout lire" and "Aléatoire": the list and its playback go last video first, numbers counting down; saved per playlist (`reversed_playlists` in `settings.toml`). A reversed playlist loads all its pages first, with a loader and the count so far;
  - every change shows a toast, at the bottom right of the window: "Ajoutée à « … »", "Retirée de « … »", or the error.
- **Channels** (from "Toutes les chaînes"): the subscribed channels as a grid of 168 px tiles (96 px round avatar, name, subscribers), a red ring and a soft surface on hover, a filter field in the header.
- **YouTube notifications**: YouTube's section of the bell's panel (see the top bar), signed in only; its unseen count is part of the bell's badge. The section titles in muted semibold ("Important", "Autres notifications"), and rows of a red dot for unread ones, the channel's 40 px round avatar, the message (three lines at most) over its muted age, and an 86 px thumbnail on the right; rows get a soft surface on hover. Skeleton rows while it loads, a spinner at the end while the next page loads.
- **Notification bell** (subscribed channels: right of the channel on the watch page, top right of the channels page's tiles): a ghost button whose icon is the level, `bell-ring` for "Toutes", `bell` for "Personnalisées", `bell-off` for "Aucune". It opens a menu of the levels the channel offers, the current one red with a check. A pick changes the icon at once, a spinner replaces it while YouTube answers, then a toast; it goes back if YouTube refuses.
- **Watch page**: the 16:9 player, the title, the channel (red on hover) and, when the user is subscribed, its notification bell (see below), the rating pill (like | dislike buttons side by side, each with its RYD count and "Likes" / "Dislikes", red when it is the user's rating), the red ratio bar under it and the RYD attribution line, which links to returnyoutubedislike.com, the description card, then the comments (each with a thumb and its like count under the text, as on YouTube, display only). The related videos are a 400 px column of compact cards that get a soft surface on hover. **Theater mode** ("Mode cinéma", a button left of fullscreen and the T key, saved as `theater` in `settings.toml`): the player spans the content panel on black, square except for its top-left corner, which follows the panel's rounded corner, above the two columns; its height is 16:9 of the width but leaves 120 px under it for the title (at least 240 px), and mpv letterboxes the picture.
- **Settings** (the gear): one page, "Réglages", then Général (folded menu), Lecteur (shared: autoplay and countdown, remember the volume, seek steps, hardware acceleration), a part per space under its name with a 3×20 px bar in its accent and a chevron, folding it on a click: the open space's first and unfolded, the others folded; each part's controls are drawn in its space's accent (`theme::tinted`) (YouTube: Compte, then language and region, default quality; Jellyfin: Compte with the server, address, user, device and sign-out, Lecture, Langues et sous-titres, Accueil), Stockage, À propos. Sections of rounded rows on `surface`, title and description on the left, small choice chips or a switch on the right (the selected one in the accent).
- **YouTube account** (in the settings): a status card (icon, state, cookie file, sign-out), then the export steps, numbered on discs in the accent, and the import button.
- **YouTube search**: the page title, a 640 px field (the search runs on Enter), then the results as list rows (videos as in list mode; a channel as its 136 px avatar centered in the thumbnail's place, its name, handle and subscribers), the next pages loading near the end.
- **Jellyfin login**: a 420 px card on `surface` (logo, "Jellyfin", "Connectez-vous à votre serveur Jellyfin"), the server, user name and masked password fields, "Se connecter" and "Utiliser Quick Connect" (the code in 40 px bold while waiting); errors in red under it.
- **Jellyfin home**: a 440 px banner (up to five titles to resume or watch next, one per series): the backdrop, darkened on the left and faded into the page at the bottom, the logo (else the title), a line in the accent (S1:E2 · name · "N min restantes", or the year), three lines of synopsis, "Reprendre"/"Lire" and "Détails" buttons, dots at the bottom right (the active one 24 px wide); then rows of cards that scroll sideways: "Reprendre" and "À suivre" (288 px 16:9 thumbnails, a click plays) and "Derniers ajouts dans …" (192 px posters, a click opens the title). Arrows appear on a row's sides on hover.
- **Jellyfin cards**: 160 px (libraries, search) or 192 px 2:3 posters and 288 px thumbnails, 8 px radius, the title and one muted line (year, or S1:E2 · episode) on one line each, so a row or a grid keeps one height; a progress bar inset at the bottom (0.5 to 99.5 %), a check on a dark disc for watched items or the count of unwatched episodes on the accent at the top right, a play button on hover for cards that play; on an episode card that plays, the series name is a link of its own. Episode stills are blurred and synopses hidden when "Masquer les spoilers" is on.
- **Jellyfin library**: the title (28 px bold) and the count ("168 titres"), then a virtual grid of 160 px posters, as many columns as the page holds, the leftover width spread between them.
- **Jellyfin title page**: a 540 px backdrop banner with the logo (else a 40 px title), year · duration · rating badge · star and score · series status, genre pills, the play button ("Lire", "Reprendre", or for a series "Lire E3"/"Reprendre E5") and four lines of synopsis; for a series, the seasons as a pivot underlined in the accent, then episode rows (a 224 px still with the progress bar and a play button on hover, "N. title", "Vu", the duration, "Reprendre à m:ss", two lines of synopsis).
- **Jellyfin search**: the page title, a 560 px field (the search runs 300 ms after typing pauses), the titles as posters, then "Épisodes" as thumbnails.
- **Jellyfin player**: a page of its own in the content panel (`Layout::Page`): back, the title and its second line over the picture, the window buttons left to the shell; Escape and Backspace go back, the wheel sets the volume, F gives fullscreen.
- **Twitch** (violet accent `#9146FF`, `TW` badge): signed out, only the sign-in card (the device code in large letters, twitch.tv/activate opened). Signed in, the sidebar has "Suivies", "Parcourir", then "En direct": the followed channels on air (avatar, name, category). Stream cards are 16:9 previews with a red "EN DIRECT" badge and the viewers, widened to fill the row; categories are tall box art; a channel's page has its header, an "EN DIRECT" banner with "Regarder" when it is on air, and "Vidéos" / "Clips" tabs.
- **Twitch watch page**: the player (16:9) with the channel's header under it (avatar, title, the channel's name in the accent, opening its page, category · viewers · uptime) and, for a live, the chat on the right (for a VOD, its replay, titled "Rediffusion du chat"), 340 px wide, on the page's whole height. In theater mode, as on Twitch, the player fills the whole page but the chat's column (the video letterboxed) and the chat runs on the page's whole height, flush against it, with no gap nor frame; the header is hidden (`ui::chat::watch_layout`).
- **Chat panel** (`ui::chat::ChatPanel`, any space): a bordered card on `surface`, "Chat du live" (or another title, `ChatPanel::titled`) with, when the room has modes, a muted "i" icon on the right whose hover card (200 ms) lists them under "Modes du chat", the connection's state under it while not connected. Messages are 14 px lines: badges (18 px), the name in bold in its color (lightened when too dark), " : ", then words and emotes (28 px high, animated ones playing) wrapping as one paragraph; mentions semibold, links underlined in the accent; `/me` in the author's color and italic; deleted messages greyed; highlighted ones tinted with the accent; subscriptions and raids in a box with an accent edge and their text in muted semibold. Scrolled up, the list stays put and a "Reprendre le défilement · N nouveaux messages" button floats at the bottom.
- **Twitch notifications**: Twitch's section of the bell's panel, signed in: the followed channels that went live while Peeby runs (the accent dot while unseen, the 40 px avatar, "<name> est en direct", the title on two lines, category · age); a click opens the live.
- **Empty and error states**: centered, an icon on a soft red disc, a title, a body and the action (import cookies, retry).
- GPUI clips children to rectangles, so the radius is set on the image itself (`image_fill(…, radius, …)`), not only on its container. That is also why cards do not zoom on hover.

## Motion

Discreet and short; nothing loops except skeletons and spinners.

| What | How | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Card hover | Its content rises 4 px inside a box that stays in place (a moving box could slip out from under the pointer and loop), a red ring and a play button fade in, the title turns red, shadow | 150 ms |
| Related card hover | A soft surface and a play button fade in, the title turns red | 150 ms |
| Image load | The image fades in over its BlurHash (Jellyfin) or the flat surface | 200 ms |
| Jellyfin banner | The next backdrop cross-fades over the previous one, the text slides in from the left; it turns every 8 s, not while hovered | 700 ms, 350 ms |
| Row arrows | Scroll by 80 % of the row's width, easing out | 280 ms |
| Page change | Fades in and rises 8 px (not the watch page: the video surface is an external texture, and leaving fullscreen would replay it) | 200 ms |
| Loading | gpui-component skeletons shaped like the content (grid cards, title and channel, related cards), a spinner at the end of a list | — |
| Busy button, list end | `ui::loader`: a faint ring with a round-capped arc turning inside it, stretching to 270° then shrinking to 12° as it turns (Material style), drawn on a canvas from a shared clock | 1.4 s per stretch, 2 s per turn |
| Skeleton to content | `motion::crossfade` on the feeds, Jellyfin's home and title pages | 300 ms |

`ui::motion` comes from peeby. GPUI only animates elements as they appear (`with_animation`); for state changes such as hover, `motion::tween` keeps a value per element id and eases it toward its target, requesting frames until it arrives. Ids must be unique per list (`card-…`, `related-…`): two elements with one id share GPUI's element state, so their hover flags overwrite each other on every frame and the card jumps up and down. Feeds therefore drop the videos YouTube repeats (the subscriptions feed repeats several per page).

GPUI takes a single `on_hover` listener per element (a second one is a debug assertion): on cards, the hover flag and the stream prefetch share it, so the prefetch keeps playback within the 1–2 s budget.
