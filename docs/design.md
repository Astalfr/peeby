# Design

Peeby looks like a Windows 11 app with a neutral dark theme. It takes peeby's visual system as it is (Fluent shapes, Mica chrome, a single accent at a time, discreet motion), the accent being the open space's: YouTube's red, Jellyfin's blue ([ADR 0006](decisions/0006-peeby-visual-system.md), [ADR 0007](decisions/0007-multi-space-app.md)). The values live in `crates/ui/src/theme.rs`. Pages use `theme::…` instead of hard-coded colors, and gpui-component widgets get the same palette through `theme::apply`. Peeby's own description is `D:\DEV\peeby\docs\design.md`.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `base` | `#0B0B0C` + 5 % of the accent | Window chrome without Mica |
| Mica veil | `base` at 55 % | Over Mica, so the chrome stays dark |
| `background` | `#121214` + 5 % of the accent | Content panel, pages |
| `surface` | `#1C1C1F` + 5 % of the accent | Cards, settings rows, description, inputs |
| `surface_hover` | `#27272B` + 5 % of the accent | Hovered surfaces, skeletons |
| `border` | `#2F2F34` | Borders, separators |
| `foreground` | `#F2F2F3` | Text |
| `muted` | `#A1A1AA` | Secondary text |
| `subtle` | `#71717A` | Section labels, fine print |
| `accent` | `#FF0000` (YouTube), `#00A4DC` (Jellyfin) | Primary buttons, progress, selection, play buttons, active item |
| `accent_hover` | `#FF3333`, `#33B6E3` | Hovered accent elements and titles |
| `accent_soft` | the accent at 14 % | Notice discs, step numbers, selected rows |
| `muted_audio` | `#E5484D` (any space) | Muted parts of a Twitch VOD on the seek bar: full where played, at 60 % ahead; "Son coupé" in the hover preview |

The backgrounds and surfaces (`base`, the Mica veil, `background`, `surface`, `surface_hover`) take 5 % of the open space's accent, so each space has its own faint hue and hovered rows match it; borders stay neutral, and so does the opening animation's background (`neutral_background`), which belongs to no space.

One accent at a time, the open space's (`theme::set_accent`; the settings page draws each space's part in its own): it marks what can be acted on or what is selected. Everything else stays neutral, so the thumbnails carry the color.

## Window and layout

- **Logo** ("Onglets", [logo/](logo/README.md)): the mascot's portrait on a rounded card tilted left with a white edge, over three cards fanned out behind it like tabs, one per space in tab order (Twitch violet `#9146ff` at the back, Jellyfin blue `#00a4dc`, YouTube red `#ff4e45` right behind the portrait). It is fixed: the open space does not change it. `assets/logo.png` (512 px, rendered by `scripts/render-logo.ps1`) is drawn in the top bar, scaled on the CPU to the exact device-pixel size (`assets::logo`, GPUI has no mipmaps); `assets/peeby.ico` (16 to 256 px) is embedded in the exe by `build.rs` (`embed-resource`), and GPUI takes the window and taskbar icon from it (resource ID 1).
- **Top bar** (44 px, a window drag area whose controls are occluded), the same in every space: a menu button (burger) that folds the sidebar, the back and forward buttons (`arrow-left`, `arrow-right`, disabled when the tab's history has nothing that way; the mouse's side buttons do the same, [ADR 0019](decisions/0019-tab-history.md)) and refresh (`refresh-cw`, "Actualiser", also F5 and Ctrl+R outside fullscreen: the page shown is built again, its data read again, a video on it starts over), Peeby's mark (the 22 px logo and the name), then the **tabs** (34 px high, 140 to 220 px wide (a short title still gets 140), narrowing when the bar is full: the service's logo in its own colors, 18 px (`SpaceInfo::icon`, a colored SVG drawn by `ui::assets::color_svg`), and the page's title, cut with an ellipsis and whole in the tooltip; the open tab has a light background, a semibold title and a 2 px line in its accent underneath; a "×" shows on the open tab and on hover, and a middle click closes a tab, except the last one; a tab held and moved follows the pointer along the bar, opaque over the others, its slot left empty, and takes its neighbors' places as it passes their middles, [ADR 0028](decisions/0028-tab-windows.md)), then "+" (`plus`), a menu "Nouvel onglet" of the spaces with their logos. A middle click on a link (a card, a channel, a sidebar entry) opens it in a new tab right after the current one, without leaving it ([ADR 0015](decisions/0015-tabs.md)). On the right: search (opens the open space's search page in a new tab right after the current one, shown, tooltip "Rechercher dans YouTube" or "… Jellyfin"; disabled when the space has none, e.g. Jellyfin signed out), the audio output (`speaker`), the notifications bell and the settings gear (the settings open in a tab of their own, with the gear as its icon, or show the one already open), before the window buttons. A video opens from the command line (`peeby <url-or-id>`); there is no URL field.
- **Accent per space**: YouTube red `#FF0000` (hover `#FF3333`, pressed `#CC0000`, the brand red), Jellyfin blue `#00A4DC` (hover `#33B6E3`, pressed `#0090C2`, the blue of its icon), later Twitch violet. The whole app takes the open space's accent.
- **Audio output** (speaker, tooltip "Sortie audio"): a 320 px menu, "Sortie audio", then "Par défaut de Windows" and each output by its Windows name, the current one in the accent with a check, rows with a soft surface on hover; "Recherche des sorties…" under the default until the first list arrives. A pick closes it and moves every player at once.
- **Notifications** (bell): one 440 px panel, "Notifications", then a section per space that has some, titled with the space's name in its accent; the badge adds up the unread counts. Without any: "Aucune notification…". A click on a notification opens it (in the space's shown tab) and closes the panel; a middle click opens it in a new tab after the current one, in the background, and leaves the panel open. Each kind (Twitch: live, category change; YouTube: new video, live) can also show as a Windows notification: the event in bold ("<name> est en direct", "<name> est passé sur <catégorie>", "Nouvelle vidéo", "En direct"), its text, and the channel's picture cropped round on the left ([ADR 0020](decisions/0020-notifications-and-background.md)).
- **Notification area**: Peeby's icon; a click opens the list of lives (a 380 px window above the icon, a tab per service with lives, each with its logo, its accent underline and the count; a row is the live's preview (16:9, as tall as the lines beside it; Twitch's refreshed every 5 minutes, YouTube's thumbnail) with the uptime over its corner (`5:30`, `2:05:30` from an hour, every second), then the channel's 20 px picture and name, the title (whole in a tooltip on hover), and held close under it a red dot with the viewers and the category; a scrollbar per tab, each with its own position; "Ouvrir Peeby" at the bottom; it closes when it loses the focus, with Escape or with another click), a double click shows the window, the right-click menu has "Ouvrir Peeby" and "Fermer". Closing the window hides it there (setting "Rester en arrière-plan").
- **Mini-player** (ADR 0008): a borderless 400×225 window above every other one, at the bottom right of the screen, with the video alone. Dragging the video moves it; its edges and corners resize it at 16:9 (240 px wide at least, the screen's width at most) with the matching resize cursors. On hover: a dark shade at the top with two round 28 px buttons on its right ("Revenir à la page", "Fermer"), and a 48 px play/pause button in the middle.
- **Sidebar** (232 px): the open space's entries only. YouTube: Accueil, Abonnements, À regarder plus tard (that playlist's page, as a sidebar page) and Playlists (the account is in the settings). Jellyfin: Accueil, then its video libraries (film, tv or folder icon) under a "BIBLIOTHÈQUES" heading (a thin line when folded); pinned at the bottom above a separator, the user (a 24 px avatar, or their initial on a disc in the accent, the name and the server's host on a muted second line; it opens the settings) and "Se déconnecter". Signed out, Jellyfin's sidebar is empty. The subscribed channels are not listed there (hundreds of them), but on their own page. Entries are 40 px, muted until hovered; the entry of the last sidebar page up to the page shown has a light background and a 3×16 px pill in the accent on its left edge, so Abonnements stays lit on the channels and channel pages opened from it. Folded (burger button, saved as `sidebar_collapsed` in `settings.toml`), it is 56 px wide: each entry shows its icon alone, centered, with its label as a tooltip, and keeps its pill.
- **Content**: a panel with a 12 px top-left corner and a 1 px top and left border, over the chrome as in Windows 11 Settings. Pages have 32 px side margins.
- **Shapes**: 8 px radius for controls, 12 px for cards and large surfaces. Font: Segoe UI Variable Text, else Segoe UI, at 15 px.

## Motion

Discreet and short; nothing loops except skeletons and spinners. The one exception is the opening animation, which is deliberately bouncy.

| Related card hover | A soft surface and a play button fade in, the title turns red | 150 ms |
| Sidebar hover card | Fades in sliding 6 px toward its entry, and out sliding away (`motion::tween`, none with reduced motion) | 150 ms |

Once the stack is still, the portrait jumps alone (stretched when fastest, tilting at the top) with a sound (`splash-jump.wav` through `player::play_wav`: Windows' `PlaySoundW`, on the default output; the file is at 20 % of the recording's level, `PlaySoundW` having no volume) and lands back, squashing the stack and rebounding. `ui::motion` comes from peeby. GPUI only animates elements as they appear (`with_animation`); for state changes such as hover, `motion::tween` keeps a value per element id and eases it toward its target, requesting frames until it arrives. Ids must be unique per list (`card-…`, `related-…`): two elements with one id share GPUI's element state, so their hover flags overwrite each other on every frame and the card jumps up and down. Feeds therefore drop the videos YouTube repeats (the subscriptions feed repeats several per page).

The opening animation is a function of the time since its first frame (the clock starts on the first render, so a slow start skips nothing): one render draws any moment, and `cargo run -p ui --example splash --release` replays it in a loop (`PEEBY_SPLASH_AT=<s>` holds a moment). Its timings and springs are constants at the top of `crates/ui/src/splash.rs`. Only SVGs turn in GPUI (`Transformation`, applied at paint time to a sprite rasterized once at the element's size), so the cards are one mask (`ui/assets/logo/card.svg`) tinted per card, and the portrait's card, already turned, is a picture (`logo-portrait.png`) that moves and stretches. Their places come from docs/logo/onglets.html (`FIT`, `CENTER`), so the settled stack matches `logo.png`; a test checks that every layer is at rest when the cross-fade starts.

GPUI takes a single `on_hover` listener per element (a second one is a debug assertion): on cards, the hover flag and the stream prefetch share it, so the prefetch keeps playback within the 1–2 s budget.
