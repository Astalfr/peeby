# 0015: Tabs of any space, links opened in a new tab

- Status: accepted (the stack of pages per tab is now a history: [0019](0019-tab-history.md))
- Date: 2026-11-03

## Context

Until now the top bar showed one fixed tab per space (YouTube, Jellyfin, Twitch, ADR 0007), each
holding a single stack of pages. The user wants tabs as in a browser: open as many tabs of a
space as needed, close them, and open a link (a video, a channel, a title) in a new tab with a
middle click.

A space is more than its pages. YouTube has one cookie session whose rotated cookies are written
back by a single `innertube::Client` (ADR 0004). Twitch has a single-use refresh token, an hourly
validation, a sidebar poll and the bell (ADR 0010). Jellyfin has one account and its token (ADR
0009). The notifications and the settings belong to the service, not to a page. Building a page
that plays starts mpv, and that writes to the account (history, reports, positions, ADR 0013), so
a tab in the background must not play.

Options:

1. One space entity per tab, the session moved to a shared per-service entity. The pages keep
   their `WeakEntity<Space>`, but every session-level call (errors, sign-out, auth-token, track
   choices) and the sidebar data move to the new entity.
2. One space entity per service, as before, holding one stack of pages per tab. The pages hold a
   `Nav` (the space and their tab) instead of the space alone.

## Decision

Option 2. The session, the sidebar's data, the bell, the inbox and the settings part stay where
they were, once per service, so nothing is duplicated and the single-use token is never spent
twice.

- `shell::space::Tabs<R>` keeps a space's tabs: per `TabId`, a stack of `Page<R>` (route, view,
  title) and the routes still to open. It also keeps the tab the space shows (the shell's, or the
  last one of the space that showed).
- `Space` methods that concern pages take a `TabId`: `menu`, `open_entry`,
  `open_entry_in_new_tab`, `can_go_back`, `back`, `open_search`, `player`, plus `new_tab`,
  `close_tab`, `show_tab` and `title`. `info`, `searchable`, `unseen`, `settings` and
  `notifications` stay per space.
- Pages hold a `Nav<S>`: the space (through `Deref`) and `tab`. What a page opens, including
  later (the next video of a queue, the next episode), lands in its own tab, even when another tab
  shows.
- A tab opened in the background (a middle click) builds nothing: its routes wait in
  `Tab::pending` and are opened at its first render. A session change (cookie import, sign-in,
  sign-out) resets every tab of the space the same way. Nothing plays in a tab nobody looks at.
  A route opened in such a tab (a notification) joins its pending routes
  (`Tabs::queue_if_pending`): built now, it would be cleared by the pending pages. A card's
  resume position (`history::offer`) is kept per video until its page opens, however late.
- A middle click (`ClickEvent::is_middle_click`, through `on_aux_click`) opens a link in a new tab
  after the current one, in the background. Several links opened from the same tab line up after
  one another, as in a browser. The tab is named after the link (the video's title) until its page
  gives its own (`Nav::set_title`). This works on cards (`MediaCard::on_middle_click`, the series
  link of an episode card), channel tiles and links, playlist cards, Jellyfin episodes, the
  sidebar entries and the bell's notifications (the panel stays open). The top bar's search
  always opens its page in a new tab after the current one, shown (`Space::search_tab`).
- The space tells the shell with `SpaceEvent::Opened { tab, after, focus }`.
  `after: None` puts it after the tab shown. `SpaceEvent::Activate` carries a `TabId`. A notification opens in the space's shown tab, or in a
  new tab when the space has none.
- The bar shows each tab with its service's logo and its page's title, a "×" on hover (on the open
  tab, always), and "+" with a menu of the spaces. A middle click on a tab closes it. The last
  tab cannot be closed.
- At startup the spaces of the setting `startup_tabs` (by `SpaceInfo::id`; default YouTube alone,
  since 2026-11-04, chosen in the gear's General section) open one tab each; with none of them
  available, the first space opens one. Command-line videos open a YouTube tab and Twitch links a
  Twitch one, shown, whatever the setting. Tabs are not saved between runs.

## Consequences

- A video keeps playing in the mini-player when its tab is left (ADR 0008). The mini-player
  follows a tab, not a space, and closing that tab closes it.
- Two tabs of the same space never render at once. The space renders the tab the shell shows, so
  one entity per space is enough for GPUI.
- Session-wide calls (`handle_error`, `log_out`, `set_auth_token`, track choices) are unchanged.
  Navigation calls take the tab.
- Revisit if tabs must be restored at startup (save the routes, not the views), or if two tabs
  have to show side by side (that needs one view per tab).
